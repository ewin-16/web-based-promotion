📖 Our Story

Born in God's Own Country

DealKerala started with a simple observation — local businesses struggle to reach nearby customers effectively. While big brands dominate social media, the neighbourhood bakery, the family-run hotel, or the new salon down the street often go unnoticed.

We built DealKerala to bridge that gap. A platform where any business — big or small — can post their offers, promotions, and advertisements to reach customers right in their district.

From Kasaragod to Thiruvananthapuram, we're helping businesses grow one deal at a time.
