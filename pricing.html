❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will my ad go live? ▼
After payment and submission, your ad is reviewed by our team within 4–6 hours. Once approved it goes live immediately and runs for the selected duration.
What image formats are accepted for the poster? ▼
We accept JPG, PNG and WebP images up to 5MB. For best results, use high-resolution images (at least 1200×600px for banners).
Can I run my ad in multiple districts? ▼
Yes! You can post separate ads for different districts. Each district placement is priced individually.
Is there a refund policy? ▼
If your ad is rejected during review, you will receive a full refund. Once an ad is live, we do not offer refunds but can pause/reschedule on request.
